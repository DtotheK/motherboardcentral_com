How to Choose the Right Motherboard

Match Your CPU

Ensure the motherboard socket matches your processor. Intel uses LGA 1700 for 12th-14th Gen, AMD uses AM5 for Ryzen 7000 series.

Consider Your Budget

B-series chipsets offer the best value. Z/X-series unlock overclocking and more PCIe lanes for enthusiasts.

Plan for the Future

DDR5 and PCIe 5.0 support ensure your build stays relevant. Consider upgrade paths when choosing.
