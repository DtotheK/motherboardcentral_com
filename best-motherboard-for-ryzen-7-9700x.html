Overview

The AMD Ryzen 7 9700X hits the sweet spot of the Zen 5 lineup — 8 cores and 16 threads with improved IPC that delivers excellent gaming and productivity performance. Its efficient 65W TDP makes it easy to cool and means you don't need the most expensive motherboard VRM.

For the 9700X, X870 motherboards offer the best balance of features and value, with WiFi 7 and PCIe 5.0 support. B650 boards are also excellent choices if you want to save money — the 9700X is fully compatible thanks to AM5's forward compatibility. You won't need more than 14 power stages for this efficient chip.

What to Look For

Must-Haves

  • X870 or B650 chipset on AM5
  • 12+ power stages (easy for the 65W 9700X)
  • DDR5 support with 4 DIMM slots
  • PCIe 5.0 x16 slot for GPU
  • WiFi 6E or WiFi 7

Nice-to-Haves

  • USB4 (X870E only)
  • 3+ M.2 NVMe slots
  • 2.5G LAN
  • Good audio codec
  • Micro-ATX option for compact builds
