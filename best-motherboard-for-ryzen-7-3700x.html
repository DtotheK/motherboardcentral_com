Overview

The AMD Ryzen 7 3700X brought 8 cores and 16 threads to the mainstream at a price point that disrupted the market. With a 65W TDP and boost clocks up to 4.4 GHz, it's efficient, capable, and still handles modern games and productivity workloads respectably.

Running on AM4, the 3700X works with B550 and X570 motherboards. B550 is the sweet spot for most builders, adding PCIe 4.0 support at a reasonable price. X570 boards offer more PCIe 4.0 lanes and additional USB connectivity, making them better for workstation use with multiple NVMe drives.

The 3700X's efficient 65W TDP means even basic B550 boards run it effortlessly. Consider upgrading the CPU to a Ryzen 5 5600X (better gaming) or Ryzen 7 5800X (better multi-threaded) on the same board — AM4's best value proposition.

What to Look For

Must-Haves

  • B550 or X570 chipset
  • DDR4 support with 4 DIMM slots
  • PCIe 4.0 x16 slot for GPU
  • At least 2x M.2 NVMe slots
  • Good rear I/O with USB 3.2

Workstation Tips

  • X570 if you need multiple PCIe 4.0 M.2 drives
  • B550 is fine for gaming with 1 PCIe 4.0 M.2
  • The 3700X's 65W TDP needs minimal VRM
  • Upgrade path: Ryzen 5800X or 5800X3D on the same board
  • 16GB RAM minimum; 32GB for content creation
