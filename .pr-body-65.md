Two extra edge cases are covered because they are silent-failure risks the criteria imply but do not name:

- `an empty third slot does not count as a differing value` — with a third slot added but unfilled, its `--` must not keep an otherwise identical row on screen. Verified red-green: removing the null-slot filter makes this test fail.
- `the toggle stylesheet does not defeat the hidden attribute` — `.compare-diff-toggle` sets `display: flex`, which would beat the UA's `[hidden]` rule and silently break the hide-below-2-boards behaviour. The rule and its guard are asserted together.
- `the toggle is not created on pages without a comparison table` — `main.js` runs site-wide; this boots it against a page with no compare elements and asserts nothing is injected.
