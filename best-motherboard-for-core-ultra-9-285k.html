Overview

The Intel Core Ultra 9 285K is Intel's flagship Arrow Lake desktop processor, featuring a hybrid architecture with performance and efficiency cores, up to 5.7 GHz boost clocks, and support for DDR5 memory. As a K-series unlocked chip, it demands a motherboard with strong VRM power delivery for overclocking, robust cooling support, and the latest connectivity standards.

For the 285K, you'll want a Z890 chipset motherboard to unlock full overclocking potential. The Z890 platform brings PCIe 5.0 for both GPU and storage, WiFi 7, Thunderbolt 4, and DDR5 support up to 192GB. Budget builders can also consider B860 boards if overclocking isn't a priority — they still deliver excellent performance at a lower cost.

What to Look For

Must-Haves

  • Z890 chipset for full overclocking support
  • 16+ power stages for stable high-frequency operation
  • DDR5 support with at least 4 DIMM slots
  • PCIe 5.0 x16 slot for current and next-gen GPUs
  • Robust VRM heatsinks with active or heatpipe cooling

Nice-to-Haves

  • WiFi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4
  • Thunderbolt 4 rear ports
  • 5+ M.2 NVMe slots
  • 10G or 5G LAN
  • Dual BIOS for recovery
