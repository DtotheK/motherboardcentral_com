Overview

The Intel Core i7-14700K remains one of the most popular gaming and productivity CPUs, offering 20 cores (8P+12E), up to 5.6 GHz boost, and excellent multi-threaded performance. Running on the mature LGA 1700 platform, it benefits from a wide selection of well-tested Z790 motherboards at competitive prices.

For the 14700K, a Z790 chipset board is recommended to unlock overclocking. The 14700K can draw significant power under load (up to 253W PBP), so prioritize boards with strong VRM cooling and at least 14+ power stages. DDR5 offers a slight performance edge in productivity workloads, though DDR4 boards are available for budget builds.

What to Look For

Must-Haves

  • Z790 chipset for overclocking
  • 14+ power stages for sustained power delivery
  • Quality VRM heatsinks — the 14700K runs hot
  • DDR5 support (DDR4 if budget is tight)
  • Good rear I/O with USB 3.2 Gen 2

Nice-to-Haves

  • WiFi 6E (standard on most Z790)
  • Thunderbolt 4 for high-speed peripherals
  • 4+ M.2 slots for storage expansion
  • 2.5G LAN for fast networking
  • BIOS Flashback for easy updates
