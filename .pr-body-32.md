## Source verification

Both XDA articles were fetched and checked against the page this run:

- **25 January 2026** — Ryzen 5 7600X, 32 GB DDR5-6000: ~10s with EXPO off, ~53s with EXPO on, roughly halved with MCR. Confirmed, including the reader comment about a board that would not POST after MCR held onto bad cached training data.
- **16 February 2026** — 45–50s down to 10–20s with MCR + Power Down Enable; random BSODs and failure to POST as the risk; clearing CMOS as the recovery path. Confirmed.

The two articles describe Power Down Enable differently — powering down idle RAM (January) vs lowering idle RAM voltage (February). The page states both and declines to pick a mechanism.
