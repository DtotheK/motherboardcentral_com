Add AM5 slow-boot guide: memory training and Memory Context Restore

We carry a large AM5 catalogue, and the most common complaint from people
who have just built on those boards is a 40-60 second black screen with the
DRAM light on once EXPO is enabled. Readers reliably misdiagnose it as
faulty memory. This adds guide-am5-slow-boot.html explaining what memory
training is, why EXPO makes it rerun, and what Memory Context Restore
trades away.

We measured nothing for this page, so the sourcing discipline is the point.
Both XDA reports were fetched and verified this run:

- 25 January 2026 -- Ryzen 5 7600X, 32 GB DDR5-6000: ~10s with EXPO off,
  ~53s with EXPO on, roughly halved with Memory Context Restore. Also
  carries the reader account of a board that would not POST after MCR held
  on to bad cached training data.
- 16 February 2026 -- readers at 45-50s reaching 10-20s with MCR plus Power
  Down Enable, with random BSODs and failure to POST as the stated risk and
  clearing CMOS as the recovery path.

Two places where the page is deliberately narrower than the issue outline:

- The issue's ASUS ROG forum source still returns HTTP 403 to automated
  fetching, confirmed again this run. Nothing on this page is attributed to
  it and it is not listed as a source, so the Q-Code 15 detail is dropped as
  the issue permits. The POST-code section stays generic and sends readers
  to their board manual and to guide-no-post.html.
- The two XDA articles describe Power Down Enable differently (powering down
  idle RAM vs lowering idle RAM voltage), so the page states both and
  declines to pick a mechanism.

Tests encode the acceptance criteria as guards: nav and footer byte-
identical to the existing guide template, every timing figure -- in digits
or spelled out -- confined to an attributed sentence, no hands-on claims,
no AGESA version strings, MCR never presented without its instability risk
and clear-CMOS recovery path, and nothing attributed to the source we could
not read.
