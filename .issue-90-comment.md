Adding a constraint that strengthens option **(b) de-claim** or **(c) remove**, and that the body above understates.

Prior research on #24 established that hand-entered retail prices on our static pages are not merely *hard to verify* — they are **against Amazon's Associates participation requirements** (https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies). Displayed prices are permitted only when Amazon serves the data, or when it comes from PA-API / the Creators API, cached no more than 24 hours, with an adjacent timestamp and the prescribed disclaimer. A dollar figure committed to an `.html` file meets none of those conditions.

Amazon is the only retailer we link from review pages, so there is no non-Amazon fallback.

Two consequences for this issue:

1. **Option (a) "date-stamp them" may not actually be available.** A date stamp satisfies CLAUDE.md rule 5 but does not satisfy the Associates terms, and the failure mode there is the affiliate account rather than a stale number. Whoever plans this should confirm whether tier bands on a roundup page count as a "displayed price" under those terms before choosing (a).
2. **The compliant substitute is a dated launch-MSRP anchor** attributed to the manufacturer or a named outlet — an orientation figure explicitly framed as launch pricing, not as what a reader will pay today. That is worth considering as a fourth option alongside (b) and (c), since it keeps most of the reader utility of the tier table.

The `reviews.html:166-169` filter buckets are likely unaffected by this, since they are UI ranges rather than a price attached to a product, but that should be confirmed rather than assumed.
