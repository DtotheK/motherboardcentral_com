Make comparisons shareable: deep-link compare.html with ?boards=

compare.html held its whole state in a JS variable and never touched the
URL, so a comparison had no address: it could not be shared, linked into
from a review page, or survive a refresh.

Give it URL state:

- On load, ?boards=<slug>,<slug> preselects those slots in order, up to
  the existing five-slot maximum.
- Every selection, removal and slot addition rewrites the URL with
  history.replaceState - no reload, and no history entry per change.

Slugs are derived from the board name, not its index in
motherboardDatabase, so adding a board cannot silently repoint links
people have already shared. The slug is also the board's review-page
filename (review-<slug>.html), so it is reusable for future
cross-linking.

Unknown, misspelled, blank, repeated and excess slugs each degrade to an
empty slot rather than erroring, and a bare compare.html is untouched:
two empty slots, placeholder text, and no rewrite of the address bar.
