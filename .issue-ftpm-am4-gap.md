## Use the socket, not the filename

The correct selector reads the spec table rather than the URL:

    grep -lE '<td>Socket</td>[[:space:]]*<td>AM[45]' review-*.html

That returns **38** pages: the 35 from #20 plus the three above. Verified against the tree at the time of writing.
