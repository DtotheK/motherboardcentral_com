## PENDING-HUMAN boards (spec discrepancy)

**ASRock X870E Taichi** — `review-asrock-x870e-taichi.html`. Left on its
search URL; links untouched.

Our spec table says **Form Factor: ATX**. The genuine listing
[`B0DFP2Q3TM`](https://www.amazon.com/ASRock-X870E-Taichi-Channel-Motherboard/dp/B0DFP2Q3TM)
says **EATX**, and ASRock's own product listing also gives EATX. Form
factor is one of the specs the recipe compares (step 3) and is named in the
reject list, so the ASIN was not used and the spec table was not edited.

I could not fetch ASRock's spec page or manual PDF directly to confirm
first-hand — both returned empty/unreadable content — so this is flagged
rather than resolved, per the "flag in the PR rather than guess" rule.
The same ASRock sources also suggest `24+2+1` power phases and `5G LAN`
where our page says `22+2+1` and `5G+2.5G`; a human should settle all three
against ASRock's spec page in a separate content issue.

Because a spec discrepancy exists, auto-merge is **not** enabled and this
PR is labelled `needs-human` (issue step 12).
