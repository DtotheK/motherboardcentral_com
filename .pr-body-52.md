## Chipset-label note (no spec table was edited)

Amazon's chipset field carries the board's model suffix rather than the AMD chipset name on four of these listings — `B650M` for the two mATX boards, `B650I` for the Mini-ITX board, and `AMD B650` for the ROG Strix B650E-E. Our tables say `B650`, `B650`, `B650` and `B650E`.

The B650E-E case was checked against the manufacturer, per the accuracy rule. ASUS's own spec page for **both** the [B650E-E](https://rog.asus.com/motherboards/rog-strix/rog-strix-b650e-e-gaming-wifi-model/spec/) and the [B650E-I](https://rog.asus.com/motherboards/rog-strix/rog-strix-b650e-i-gaming-wifi-model/spec/) lists the chipset as `AMD B650`, while ASUS's own overview copy, Newegg's listing and the model designation all say B650E. So this is an ASUS labelling convention applied across their whole B650E line, not a fact about one board — and not a product-identity problem: both listings match our `<h1>` token for token, including the `B650E-E` / `B650E-I` suffix. Our `B650E` rows are left as they are. Flagging rather than editing, since this issue forbids spec-table changes.
