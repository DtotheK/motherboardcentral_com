## What this page deliberately does not say

The page's subject is the state of the information, not the hardware. It names the five reported
chipset tiers (Z990, Z970, W980, Q970, B960) and prints **not one leaked number** — no lane count,
no DMI figure, no Thunderbolt generation, no port count, no memory speed, no price, no retail date.
Every one of those is machine-blocked by `scripts/intel-900-series.test.mjs`, so a later edit cannot
quietly reintroduce them.

The reason is the plan's **one-source rule**, which the page states in its own voice: several
outlets repeating one leaker is one source, not several. Independence is counted at the origin. I
verified the origin — Igor's Lab names it: *"It all started in early February with a leak from the
well-known hardware leaker Jaykihn, who mentioned a 900-series chipset family for Nova Lake-S,
including LGA1954."* Posted 9 February 2026. The six links in the issue body all carry that one
table.

## What changed vs. the plan — please read this bit

Caution **C1** told me to re-check the socket claim at implementation time and demote it to
"reported" if I could not find an Intel first-party page or an earnings-call transcript.
**I could not, and the same check demoted more than just the socket.**

Verified 21 August 2026 against `newsroom.intel.com`, `intc.com` and the Q3 2025 earnings-call
transcript. Intel's own material carries **no** Nova Lake socket name, chipset name, launch date,
core count, graphics architecture, process node or branding. Igor's Lab says so outright: *"The
socket LGA1954 and the 900-series chipsets remain unconfirmed early information for now"* /
*"Intel has not confirmed this yet."*

So the **confirmed** section is now only three items, each tied to a quotable Intel source:

1. Nova Lake is a real, named Intel desktop product — Lip-Bu Tan on Intel's newsroom, 24 July 2025.
2. It succeeds Arrow Lake and targets gaming — Tan, Q3 2025 earnings call.
3. Arrow Lake / LGA 1851 is still Intel's current desktop platform — Core Ultra Series 3 (Panther
   Lake, CES 2026) names no desktop part.

LGA 1954, the five chipset tiers, DDR5-only, the second-half-of-2026 timing and the "Core Ultra
Series 4" branding all moved to **reported**, attributed. The plan's Step 2 had listed several of
those as confirmed; CLAUDE.md rule 1 and the plan's own D4 tier definitions say they cannot be.
**This is the main thing to review.**

Two further verification results worth recording:

- **C2 confirmed, and it is worse than stated.** The issue body claims "Z990: Thunderbolt 5 (up to
  120 Gbps)". The original leak table says USB4 / Thunderbolt 4, and no bandwidth figure. The issue
  body also disagrees with the leak on which tier lacks PCIe 5.0 lanes. **Issue #29's body must not
  be used as a source by any future page.**
- **C5 resolved in the direction the planner expected.** Intel Core Ultra Series 3 / Panther Lake is
  mobile and edge only. There is no Series 3 desktop content gap.
