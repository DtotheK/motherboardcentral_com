Overview

The AMD Ryzen 5 3600 was one of the best-selling CPUs of all time — and it's easy to see why. Six cores, twelve threads, and excellent single-threaded performance made it the go-to budget gaming processor for years. On the mature AM4 platform with DDR4 RAM, it remains a viable option for budget builds.

The 3600's modest 65W TDP means virtually any AM4 motherboard can run it without VRM concerns. A B550 board is the best match, providing PCIe 4.0 support for your GPU and NVMe drives. B450 boards are even cheaper but limited to PCIe 3.0 — fine for most gaming at 1080p.

Considering an upgrade? The Ryzen 5 5600X drops into the same AM4 board and offers ~20% better gaming performance thanks to Zen 3's IPC gains. Or jump to AM5 with the 7800X3D for a massive leap.

Building an AMD system? Two vulnerabilities in the TPM 2.0 reference code behind AMD's firmware TPM were published on 11 August 2026 (AMD-SB-7064, CVE-2026-6726 and CVE-2026-6727). Both need local privileged access, so neither is remotely exploitable, and this is not a recall. The fix ships as a motherboard BIOS update rather than through Windows Update, and coverage varies by board. Our fTPM caveat explainer covers what to check before you enable fTPM.

Our Top 4 Picks

MSI MAG B550 Tomahawk

Best Overall

12+2+1 phases · 2.5G LAN · 2x M.2 · Future-proof VRM

The gold standard for AM4 budget builds. The 12+2+1 VRM is massively overkill for the 3600, meaning this board will handle any AM4 upgrade you throw at it (even a 5800X3D). 2.5G LAN is a nice bonus, and the 2x M.2 slots cover storage needs. Bulletproof reliability from MSI's best AM4 board.

What to Look For

Must-Haves

  • B550 chipset (best value with PCIe 4.0)
  • DDR4 support with 4 DIMM slots
  • At least 1x M.2 NVMe slot
  • Updated BIOS for Zen 2 support (most ship ready)
  • 2.5G or Gigabit LAN

Budget Tips

  • The 3600 is so efficient that any B550 VRM handles it
  • Skip WiFi if you use ethernet — save $20-30
  • Micro-ATX boards are often cheaper
  • B550 gives you a drop-in upgrade path to the 5600X or 5800X3D
  • Reuse existing DDR4 RAM to save money
