Fix homepage star ratings that disagreed with their review pages

The MSI MAG B760 Tomahawk WiFi (4.6) and ASUS ROG Strix X670E-E Gaming
WiFi (4.7) cards on index.html drew four filled stars plus an outline,
while the same boards' review pages drew five. Tapping either card
showed the reader a different star count one click later.

The sitewide rule -- verified across all 70 review-*.html pages -- is
>= 4.6 draws five filled stars, <= 4.5 draws four filled plus one
outline. index.html was using a different cutoff. Both cards now follow
the sitewide rule. The numeric ratings are untouched.

Adds scripts/homepage-star-ratings.test.mjs, which checks every homepage
review card against both the sitewide rule and its own review page.
