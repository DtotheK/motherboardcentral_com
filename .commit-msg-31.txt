Add BIOS update guide (including flashing with no CPU installed)

New page guide-bios-update.html, built from the existing guide-*.html
template with the nav and footer copied unchanged. Covers when an update
is actually warranted, finding the board model and current version, the
in-BIOS flash tools, the no-CPU USB flash button, USB stick preparation
and file naming, why an interrupted flash is unrecoverable, what a flash
resets, recovery options, and what to do on boards without a flash button.

Sourcing is the point of this page, because a wrong filename can leave a
reader with a dead board. Per-vendor rules are only stated where they
could be read at the vendor's own source:

- ASUS: USB BIOS FlashBack FAQ (asus.com/support/faq/1038568) for the
  FAT16/32 MBR single-sector requirement, the .CAP file and BIOSRenamer,
  the three-second button press, the three-blink start signal, the
  five-seconds-then-solid failure signal, the eight-minute ceiling and
  the verbatim do-not-interrupt list. A second ASUS FAQ (1053582) is
  cited for models that take .AS rather than .CAP.
- MSI: MSI's own BIOS user guide PDF for the MSI.ROM rename, the root
  directory placement, both power connectors with no CPU or memory
  installed, the rear Flash BIOS Port, and the LED-off completion
  signal, plus the M-FLASH sequence.
- GIGABYTE and ASRock: no filename is printed. GIGABYTE's site blocks
  automated retrieval and its naming rule has changed across board
  generations; ASRock publishes its flash procedure as image-only PDFs.
  Both sections say so and send the reader to their board's own support
  page, as the issue requires.

The BitLocker warning is sourced to Microsoft's own recovery
documentation, which lists a BIOS or UEFI firmware upgrade as a trigger
and recommends suspending protection for a planned update.

No AGESA or BIOS version string is named anywhere, per the conflict
flagged in #20. No hands-on claim is made; the page states plainly that
it is based on published manufacturer documentation.

scripts/guide-bios-update.test.mjs encodes the issue's acceptance
criteria as 29 tests, including the sourcing rules: a rename rule may
only appear in a section that links the vendor page it came from, and
the unconfirmed vendors must point at the reader's own board page. That
test caught the MSI rename rule being stated without its citation.

Other files touched are the two the issue allows: one card in
guides.html and one <loc> in sitemap.xml.
