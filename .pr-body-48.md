## ⚠️ Spec observations for a human — not changed by this PR

While reading spec tables for the comparison I noticed discrepancies that are
outside this issue's scope but affect reader trust (CLAUDE.md rule 1). Flagging
rather than guessing:

- **`review-asrock-b760m-itx-d4-wifi.html`** — our table: `LAN 2.5G`, `WiFi 6`.
  ASRock's spec page: Gigabit LAN (RTL8111H), Wi-Fi 6E.
- **`review-gigabyte-b760m-ds3h-ax-ddr4.html`** — our table: `WiFi 6`.
  GIGABYTE's spec page: Wi-Fi 6E.
- **`review-asrock-b760m-pro-rs-d4.html`** — our table: `LAN 1G`, `SATA Ports 2`,
  `Memory DDR4, 2 DIMM slots, up to 64GB`. The search index of ASRock's spec page
  reports 2.5G Dragon LAN (RTL8125BG) and 4x SATA3. **This board was still
  converted**, because its socket, chipset, memory generation, form factor and
  WiFi variant — the fields the issue's recipe compares — all match the listing.

ASRock and GIGABYTE spec pages are JS-rendered or 403 to direct fetch, so these
came from search-engine indexes of those pages rather than a direct read. Worth
a human confirming against the manufacturer pages before any edit.
