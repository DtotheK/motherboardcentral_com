Add review pages for the GIGABYTE B450M D3HP and B450M D3HP WIFI6E

GIGABYTE announced both boards on 14 August 2026. The site had broad AM4
coverage but no B450 board records at all, while the Ryzen 3000/5000
buying guides we already rank for point straight at this segment.

Two new pages, built from GIGABYTE's own published specification text and
corroborated against Igor's Lab, ThinkComputers, TweakTown and VideoCardz:

- AM4 / B450 / Micro-ATX, Ryzen 5000 / 4000 / 3000 support
- 4 x DDR4 DIMM, up to 128GB; digital 4+2-phase VRM
- 2 x M.2 - one CPU-connected at PCIe 3.0 x4, one chipset-connected at
  PCIe 2.0 x2; 4 x SATA 6 Gb/s
- Rear I/O: 4 x USB 3.2 Gen 1, 3 x USB 2.0, 1 x DisplayPort, 2 x HDMI
- Realtek Gigabit Ethernet on both models; the WIFI6E adds a Realtek
  RTL8852CE for Wi-Fi 6E (2.4/5/6 GHz) and Bluetooth 5.4 alongside the
  wired port rather than in place of it

The second M.2 slot is the trap in this spec sheet, so it is called out
in the table, in its own "Storage: the M.2 catch" section, and in the
cons list on both pages. It is never described as a second full-speed
NVMe slot.

What the pages deliberately do NOT carry, because GIGABYTE has not
published it: a price, an on-sale date, an audio codec, a memory speed
list, or an Amazon link (no ASIN confirmed - CLAUDE.md rule 3). Each page
has a "What GIGABYTE has not published" section saying so. There is no
star rating either: we have not tested these boards, and every analytical
claim is framed as based on published specifications.

scripts/gigabyte-b450m-d3hp.test.mjs encodes the issue's acceptance
criteria as assertions - the verified spec values, cross-page agreement
on every shared field, the PCIe 2.0 x2 slot in both table and prose, and
the whole "do not publish" list - so a later edit cannot quietly
reintroduce a fabricated spec.

reviews.html gets both cards, a B450 chipset filter option and a corrected
board count. The cards carry data-price="-1" so a board with no published
price cannot fall into the "Under $150" filter bucket. No nav, footer or
layout markup changed.
