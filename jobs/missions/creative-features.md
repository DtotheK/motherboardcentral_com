Mission: propose improvements nobody asked for — the ambitious ideas.
1. Browse the site (ls *.html, read 2-3 pages) as three readers: a
   first-time builder, an upgrade researcher, a deal hunter. What would
   each wish existed?
2. Research what works across hardware-content sites: interactive tools,
   calculators, visual explainers, formats.
3. File:
   - Small polish (tooltip glossary, spec-highlight, table sorting) →
     agent-ok, title "Polish: <thing>"
   - New features, tools, page types, redesigns → needs-plan, title
     "Feature idea: <thing>" — body: the reader problem, evidence of
     demand (links), rough scope, success criteria.
4. One idea you'd defend in person beats three you wouldn't. Never file
   without evidence someone wants it.
End with a one-paragraph summary of what you filed and why.
