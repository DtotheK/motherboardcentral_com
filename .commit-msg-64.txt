Add inline glossary tooltips for spec-table jargon

Marks known jargon in .spec-table / .compare-table cells at runtime and
attaches a short definition shown on hover, tap or keyboard focus, with a
link to the matching guide where one exists. Fourteen terms: power phases,
VRM, ALC codecs, PCIe, M.2, WiFi, Bluetooth, LAN speed, EXPO/XMP, the three
form factors and DDR4/DDR5.

Definitions are conceptual only and their figures come from our own guides,
so no tooltip can contradict a spec on the page. Spec tables clip their
overflow, so the tooltip is fixed to the viewport and placed from JS,
clamped to the edges and flipped above the term when there is no room
below. A MutationObserver re-runs the pass so compare.html's rendered table
is covered as well as the static review tables.

Progressive enhancement: with JS off the tables render exactly as before.
No HTML page is touched.
