## ⚠️ Please read: the issue's CPU spec is wrong, and the page does not follow it

The issue's content spec says:

> the 9800X3D is a 65 W-class TDP part with locked-down X3D behaviour

Both halves contradict the published specification. Every source I could read gives **120W default TDP**, and the 9800X3D is **unlocked for overclocking** — it is the first X3D part that is, because the second-generation V-Cache die sits beneath the cores rather than on top of them.

- Newegg product page (fetched 2026-08-20): 8-Core / 16-Threads, 8MB L2, 96MB L3, **120W** TDP, Socket AM5, "unlocked for overclocking" — https://www.newegg.com/amd-ryzen-7-9000-series-ryzen-7-9800x3d-granite-ridge-zen-5-socket-am5-desktop-cpu-processor/p/N82E16819113877

- Tom's Hardware review: https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/amd-ryzen-7-9800x3d-review-devastating-gaming-performance
