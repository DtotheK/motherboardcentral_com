Add board record + review: GIGABYTE B760M GAMING WIFI6E DDR4 GEN5

A refreshed LGA 1700 Micro-ATX board that pairs DDR4 memory with a
PCIe 5.0 x16 graphics slot. We already cover two DDR4 B760 boards and
rank for best-motherboard-for-i5-12400f, and none of those boards
offer Gen5, so this fills a real gap for budget LGA 1700 builders who
do not want to buy DDR5.

Specifications published, each one attributed to this exact SKU:

- LGA 1700 / Intel B760 / Micro-ATX, Intel 12th / 13th / 14th Gen Core
- 2 x DDR4 DIMM, up to 64GB, dual channel, XMP
- 1 x PCIe 5.0 x16 (reinforced, quick release), 1 x PCIe 3.0 x1
- 2 x M.2, both PCIe 4.0 x4; 4 x SATA 6 Gb/s
- 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet plus Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth, not one instead
  of the other
- Hybrid 6+2+1 phase VRM; 8-channel HD audio

The two traps the issue calls out each get their own section rather
than a single line in the table. "What the Gen5 slot covers" says the
PCIe 5.0 link is the graphics slot and that both M.2 sockets are
PCIe 4.0 x4; "Memory: two slots, not four" states the 64GB ceiling and
warns against carrying the 4-slot layout across from our other B760M
pages. Both points are repeated in the cons list.

GIGABYTE sells at least four boards whose names differ from this one
by a single word, so the page carries a "What we have not verified"
section listing what was left blank rather than filled in from a
sibling model's spec sheet: the rear panel layout and video outputs,
the audio codec, the Bluetooth version, the memory speed list and a
price. There is no release date (GIGABYTE has published no launch
press release), no price, no star rating and no Amazon link, since no
ASIN was confirmed for this exact SKU (CLAUDE.md rule 3).

scripts/gigabyte-b760m-gaming-wifi6e-ddr4-gen5.test.mjs encodes the
issue's acceptance criteria as assertions, including regex guards that
fail if a later edit ever implies Gen5 storage, reintroduces the
128GB / 4-DIMM layout, adopts the GAMING X phase count, or links the
GAMING X specification page.

reviews.html gets one card, filterable as LGA 1700 / B760 / Micro-ATX,
carrying data-price="-1" so a board with no published price cannot
fall into the "Under $150" bucket. No nav, footer or layout markup
changed.

The B450 suite's site-wide card-count assertion is replaced with a
check that its own two cards are still present. That total belongs to
whichever board page was added last, so as written it would have
failed on every future board addition.
