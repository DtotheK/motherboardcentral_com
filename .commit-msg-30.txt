Add no-POST troubleshooting guide (debug LED meanings)

The site had no troubleshooting content at all -- every page was a
buying/spec explainer or a review. This adds guide-no-post.html covering
what a stuck CPU, DRAM, VGA or BOOT debug LED means and what to do about
each one.

Sourcing is the whole risk on this topic, so the page is deliberately
narrower than the issue's outline in two places:

- MSI, GIGABYTE and ASRock all block automated fetching of their support
  pages, so only ASUS's Q-LED FAQ could be read directly. Vendor-specific
  detail is therefore ASUS-attributed and quoted from that FAQ; the other
  three vendors get their feature named and their own support page linked,
  with no behavioural claim we could not trace. The secondary-source claim
  about MSI's LEDs lighting in sequence is omitted.
- The issue cited XDA for "a long AM5 first boot with the DRAM LED lit is
  normal memory training". That article covers the memory training and
  Memory Context Restore, but says nothing about the DRAM LED. The LED half
  of the claim is attributed instead to ASUS's ROG article on AM5 memory
  training (Q-CODE 15 / yellow DRAM Q-LED), and XDA is cited only for what
  it actually says.

No universal beep-code table: published tables disagree with vendor
manuals, so the page tells the reader to use their own board's manual.

Tests encode the issue's acceptance criteria as guards -- nav/footer
byte-identical to the existing guide template, every named vendor linked
to its own support page, no hands-on claims, no beep-code mapping.
